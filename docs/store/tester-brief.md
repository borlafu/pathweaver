# Closed test: what to tell testers

Text to send to anyone joining the closed track. It is written to be pasted into a message
unchanged, so it says nothing a tester would have to ask a follow-up question about.

Two things matter more than any bug they find: they must **join with the Google account they use on
their phone**, and they must **stay opted in for fourteen consecutive days**. Play counts continuous
days per tester, and anyone who leaves the programme restarts their own count — so a tester who
opts out on day ten and rejoins on day eleven has one day, not eleven.

---

## Message to send

> **Pathweaver: Pocket Realms — closed test**
>
> Thanks for helping. This is a small offline puzzle game: you place hex pipes to link springs to
> hubs, one thumb, no account, no ads, no internet needed.
>
> Two requests before anything else:
>
> 1. Join with the **same Google account you use on your phone's Play Store**. A different account
>    cannot see the test.
> 2. **Stay in the test for at least two weeks.** Google requires twelve people opted in
>    continuously for fourteen days before the game can be released publicly, and leaving early
>    resets that person's count. If you need to stop, tell me rather than opting out.
>
> To join: open `<opt-in link>` on your phone, tap **Become a tester**, then install from the Play
> Store link on the same page. It can take a few minutes to appear the first time.
>
> What is worth reporting, in rough order of usefulness:
>
> - anything that crashed, froze, or lost your progress
> - a level you could not finish and believe is impossible
> - a control that did not do what you expected
> - anything that felt unfair rather than difficult
>
> Send it however you like — a sentence and a screenshot is plenty. Please include your phone model
> if something looked wrong on screen.
>
> There is no soundtrack yet and the art is placeholder: flat colours and simple shapes. That part is
> known, so no need to report it.

---

## Recruiting notes

- Recruit **more than twelve.** Twelve is the floor Play checks against, not a target; one person
  changing phones or clearing their account can cost a fortnight.
- Collect the Google account email for each tester and add them to the group used by the closed
  track. Individual email lists in the Console work but need editing per change; a group does not.
- Note the date each tester's opt-in is confirmed. The fourteen-day clock is per tester, and the
  production-access form asks what the test covered.
- Testers cannot be recruited inside the Console. Play only checks who is opted in, so where they
  come from is unconstrained.

## Feedback log

Production access asks for a summary of what testers reported and what changed as a result, so keep
`closed-test-feedback.md` current from the first day rather than reconstructing it at the end.
