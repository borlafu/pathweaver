# Privacy Policy for Pathweaver: Pocket Realms

**Last updated: 23 August 2026**

## The short version

Pathweaver: Pocket Realms collects nothing, sends nothing, and needs no account. It has no
analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no network features of any kind. Nothing you
do in the game leaves your device.

This policy exists because Google Play requires every app to have one, not because there is
much to say.

## What the game stores on your device

Two files, in the private storage Android allocates to the app:

- **Your game in progress** — the board you are part-way through, your score, and your tokens.
- **Your progress through the campaign** — which levels you have completed.

Both stay on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them. Nothing is backed up to a server
because there is no server.

## What the game does not do

- It does not collect or transmit personal information.
- It does not use analytics or any other measurement service.
- It does not show advertising.
- It does not ask for an account, an email address, or a sign-in.
- It does not read your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.
- It does not track you across apps or websites.

## Permissions

The app requests one Android permission:

- **Vibrate** — used to produce a short buzz when a tile is placed and when a route completes.
  It cannot be used to read anything about you, and vibration can be switched off in the game's
  settings.

## Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and does not collect data from anyone, including
children. Since nothing is collected from any user, there is nothing collected from a child
user either.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds anything that collects data — a leaderboard, cloud saves,
or optional rewarded video — this policy will be updated before that version is published, and
the change will be described here rather than quietly applied.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact address listed on the game's
Google Play listing.
